Report Fraud Job
If any job asks for money without showing valid OEPL license, or asks for payment via mobile wallet for Govt job, it's fraud.

What to do:

  1. Email us at info@qadamrozgar.com with job title and company
  2. Report on official BEOE site: beoe.gov.pk with OEPL number
  3. Do NOT pay until OEPL verified on beoe.gov.pk
