How to Verify Gulf Job (Must Do Before Payment)

  1. Ask employer for OEPL (Overseas Employment Promoter License) Number — format: OEPL-XXXX
  2. Go to beoe.gov.pk → List of OEPs (official Govt site)
  3. Search license number — If not found in official list, it's FRAUD — don't pay
  4. Check office address physically — Visit office if possible before payment
  5. Never pay full fee upfront — BEOE allows limited service charge only as per law
  6. Take receipt of any payment with OEPL number mentioned

For Govt Jobs Safety

Apply only on official portal: njp.gov.pk, fpsc.gov.pk, ppsc.gop.pk, etc. No Govt job requires payment via mobile wallet. If someone asks, it's fraud.
