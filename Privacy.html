Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 15 Sep 2026 • QADAMROZGAR.COM

1. Info Portal Only

QADAMROZGAR.COM is an information aggregation portal. We do NOT collect fees for Govt jobs. We do NOT store CVs on server — all ATS checking happens in your browser only (localStorage).

2. Data Collection

We collect: Search queries (anonymous), Saved jobs (localStorage only in your browser), Apply count (localStorage). No personal data is sent to any server. Everything stays in your device.

3. Cookies

Only essential cookies for pagination and saving preferences. No tracking cookies. No third-party tracking.

4. Gulf Jobs Safety

For Gulf jobs, always verify OEPL license on beoe.gov.pk. We are not a recruitment agency. We do not charge any fees. We are only an info portal.

5. Contact for Data Deletion

For data deletion: Email info@qadamrozgar.com — We will guide you to clear your browser localStorage. No server data to delete.

⚠️ BEOE Safety Warning

Verify Overseas Employment Promoter License Number on beoe.gov.pk before any payment. QadamRozgar does not guarantee job placement and never asks for money. If someone asks for money on our name, it's fraud.
