🎯 EXPERT - Dusron ke liye Apply kaise karen? (Aapki site ka Business Model)

Step 1 - Lead Generation: Log aapki site pe Direct Apply karte hain (CV + Phone) → Yahan save hota hai (Owner Only).
Step 2 - Aap Recruiter Bante Ho: Har lead pe 3 buttons: WhatsApp (candidate ko contact), 📤 Forward (company ko forward), Download CV.
Step 3 - Forward kaise?
- Gulf jobs: BEOE OEPL check karo (beoe.gov.pk), phir candidate ka CV + aapki agency details se OEPL portal pe apply.
- Local jobs: Company ke career page / HR email pe candidate ka CV forward (email draft auto-banega).
- Aap commission / service charge le sakte ho — lekin Govt jobs pe fee lena illegal hai, sirf Gulf private allowed (with OEPL).
Step 4 - Database Shift: Export JSON → Firebase/Supabase → wahan se auto-email to companies.
📦 Database Shift Guide (Future):
1. Firebase/Supabase: Is JSON ko wahan import karo — `appliedJobs` array hi leads hai.
2. Current: Sab `localStorage` me hai — `appliedJobs` key. Owner ke browser me safe hai.
3. Next Step: Jab chahe backend banao — `fetch('/api/leads', {method:'POST', body: JSON.stringify(lead)})` add karna hai `submitDirectApplication()` me — 2 lines ka kaam.
4. Lead Gen Use: In numbers ko WhatsApp Broadcast, SMS, ya Gulf agencies ko forward kar sakte ho — yehi aapka asset hai.
5. CV Files: Choti files base64 me save hoti hain, badi files ka naam + size save hota hai. DB me shift karte waqt S3/Cloudinary pe upload karna.
